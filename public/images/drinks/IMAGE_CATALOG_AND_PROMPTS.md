## Art Direction Guidelines
- **Lighting:** Warm directional key light, soft dark-emerald green ambient shadows, warm amber/copper rim highlights.
- **Atmosphere:** Egyptian specialty café aesthetic with dark timber counters, warm tungsten glow, natural wooden textures.
- **Glassware/Vessels:** BU-GA Café branded porcelain demitasse cups, highball glasses, clear ribbed glasses with condensation, or branded takeaway cups.
- **Composition:** 3:4 portrait orientation, centered beverage, shallow depth of field (f/1.8 to f/2.8), macro beverage detail.
- **Exclusions:** Strictly NO people, NO background patrons, NO text overlays, NO promotional fonts, NO price labels.
